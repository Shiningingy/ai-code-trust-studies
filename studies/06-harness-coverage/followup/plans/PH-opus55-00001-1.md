## What could cause a miss
- A too-strict regex: lowercase names, "Org" vs "Organization", lowercase event IDs, multi-word cities, or facts stated in the first person.
- Facts recorded only under an alias or initial that I failed to map to the full name.
- Splitting sentences wrongly on initials like "E." or "St.", which cuts a fact in half.
- Counting volunteering per mention instead of per distinct event, or missing that a fact was reported in someone else's quote.

- Crediting an ambiguous initial ("A. Diaz" when there are two A. Diazes) or a pronoun to the wrong person.
- Counting last-year or `old-event` volunteering, or treating past, planned or one-off behaviour ("used to", "no longer", "plans to", "used the bus once") as current.
- Getting a city's coastal/inland status wrong.
